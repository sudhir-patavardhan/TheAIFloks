## Known gaps (do not treat as established without further sourcing)

- No independently audited outcome exists for any hotel-group or OTA AI figure above (#10, #12, #15, #19, #21, #24, #26–27, #50, #55–56): all are company disclosures on earnings calls or newsrooms. The most-repeated figure in the domain — "customer-service cost per booking down double digits" — appears at Booking, Agoda, Expedia and Airbnb with no common definition.
- No chain has disclosed conversion, booking or revenue attribution for its conversational search / AI planner (#1, #6, #9, #10, #16); Expedia's statement that natural-language experiences "do not currently drive conversion" (#25) is the only direct evidence on the point.
- The McDonald's "1M orders / 90%" figures (#48) are franchisee social-media claims, unconfirmed by the company.
- Skift, PhocusWire, Phocuswright, CNBC, GeekWire, QSR Magazine and Hotel Online blocked automated fetches; #20, #34, #36 and #58 rest on search-verified URLs and are flagged as such. The Skift white paper (#36) is sponsored content.
- No STR/CoStar or Deloitte figure isolates AI's contribution to 2026 hotel margins; 2026 RevPAR growth is being driven by pricing power and event demand (#47), so sell-side "AI payoff" claims (#20) are not separable from the cycle.
- Luxury/independent operators: no primary-source AI initiative from Four Seasons, Mandarin Oriental, Rosewood or Aman was found in this pass; the independent segment appears only as the object of platform decisions (#30, #35).
- Revenue-management outcomes (#10, #42, #43) are stated qualitatively ("revenue uplift") or as vendor marketing; a circulating "17% total revenue increase for AI-RMS adopters" figure could not be traced to a primary source and is excluded.
