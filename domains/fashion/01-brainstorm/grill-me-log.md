## Session 2026-08 — Target claim: "The durable AI moat in fashion is who controls the shared, verifiable data infrastructure a garment carries from factory to resale"

Candidate theses considered before grilling: (a) provenance-as-moat (broad), (b) Aura-only (narrow), (c) contrarian reversal (AI forecasting accelerates overproduction). User selected (a); (c) survives as objection-handling material inside the article, not its spine.

### Attacks raised

1. **Evidence attack** — Two hits. (i) The single most load-bearing fact — Aura's scale (40M+ products, LVMH/Prada/Cartier/OTB) — traces to exactly one source: Aura's own consortium website (sources.md #22). Tiered P, but self-interested-party-describing-own-traction is not earnings-call-grade P; zero independent confirmation of adoption depth found. (ii) Scope inflation: the evidence covers *authentication at manufacture*; "factory to resale" is an inferred extension, not an observed one.
2. **Counterexample attack, fashion-specific** — Blockchain-for-luxury-provenance has a hype-then-fizzle track record (2018–19 pilots; outside knowledge, not in sources.md — flagged as unciteable). What separates Aura: (i) four *competing* conglomerates cooperating = a coordination cost new entrants can't casually replicate; (ii) EU ESPR/DPP mandate (#75) turns provenance from PR into a compliance deadline. Residual risk: the EU could accept cheap non-blockchain database solutions as compliant, commoditizing "provenance" and stranding Aura's specific implementation. Unresolved — carried into the article as a named risk.
3. **Counterexample attack, framework-level** — Payment-fraud detection (Visa/Mastercard) looks like purchased post-hoc automation that IS a durable moat. Resolution: it's not a counterexample — those models are durable because they compound on an exclusive, network-scale transaction graph competitors structurally can't access. This *reframed the thesis* (see mutation below).
4. **Constraint attack** — Aura removes the binding constraint in principle (authenticity: probabilistic post-hoc inference → ground-truth lookup at origin). But **no evidence anyone queries the registry at the point of resale** (thrift, consignment, StockX/TheRealReal-tier marketplaces — where secondhand counterfeiting actually lives). If resale still defaults to Entrupy-style inspection, the constraint is removed in theory but sitting unused. **UNRESOLVED — user redirected to drafting before answering.** The draft must not claim resale-side adoption; it must flag this as the thesis's open front.
5. **Incumbent's rebuttal** — run 2026-08, two prongs. (i) "Your centerpiece isn't an AI story — Aura is database/governance plumbing." Defense: framework step 4 ("intelligence lives upstream of where automation tools sit"); Aura is the data substrate future intelligence compounds on (payment-fraud analogy). Draft 1 pre-empts this in the payment-fraud paragraph. RESOLVED. (ii) "Execution of purchased automation IS retail's moat — everyone buys the same tools, Inditex still wins." Recommended defense: concede operational excellence is real and durable, but it's a management capability that predates AI, isn't what vendors sell, and buying the tool doesn't buy the execution; the thesis is about which *AI investments* compound. Complicating fact: our best counter-case (Zara) rests on figures our own log flags unverified (#10). **OPEN — user decision pending: add the execution-as-moat concession paragraph to revision 2, or leave it out.** Recommendation: add it.
6. **Survivorship & selection** — run 2026-08, three sub-attacks. (i) Unit-economics selection: Aura is a *luxury* phenomenon — per-item identity amortizes on a $5,000 bag, not a $12 tee. Partial survival: EU DPP mandate (#75) extends passports to all EU-sold textiles regardless of price — but cheap goods will get cheap compliance, feeding the commoditization falsifier. MUTATION REQUIRED in revision 2: state plainly the network-value story is luxury-led today and regulation (not economics) is what extends it down-market; draft 1 implies industry-wide reach too easily. (ii) Graveyard selection: Aura may be the survivor of dead 2018–19 consortia; defense (multi-conglomerate coordination) stands but is unciteable — draft correctly doesn't lean on it. Survives by not being load-bearing. (iii) The 2x2's own selection bias: "10 of 12 in PA" is based on publicly reported initiatives; empty individual-scope quadrants partly reflect reporting visibility. Already disclosed in research + artifact; logged here for completeness.
7. **Falsifiability** — run 2026-08. Survived: three named, observable falsifiers. (a) EU textiles delegated act accepts cheap self-declared databases as compliant passports → provenance commoditizes, coordination premium evaporates (already in draft 1). (b) Consortium defection: a founding member leaving Aura or shipping a competing proprietary registry kills the coordination-premium claim. (c) The Entrupy test: a post-hoc authenticator sustaining pricing power/dominance over years WITHOUT network exclusivity would prove purchased automation can be a moat here. None currently observed; all three go on the watch list.

### Claim survived / died / mutated

- **Verdict:** Mutated (three times), survives in sharpened form. All seven attack angles now run. Resolution of the two pending items (2026-08, revision 2): (a) execution-as-moat concession — implemented in compact 2–3-sentence form woven near the Zara passage (recommended form; user gave general revision direction favoring tightness but did not explicitly rule on this item — flag at final review); (b) luxury-led scope honesty — implemented. New outcome from the user's read: the resale-adoption gap (attack 4) is reframed from caveat to **open opportunity** and promoted to the public Unsolved Problems registry (`shared/unsolved-problems.md`) — the registry's first entry sourced directly from a grill session.
- **Surviving form of the claim:** *In fashion, durable AI value accrues to systems that compound on an exclusive, hard-to-replicate data network — and the clearest instance is provenance embedded at the point of manufacture (Aura Consortium), soon to be reinforced by EU Digital Product Passport regulation. Purchasable automation — forecasting SaaS, try-on widgets, trend subscriptions, post-hoc CV authentication — is the commoditizing baseline, however real its productivity gains.* Scope narrowed from "factory to resale" to "at manufacture, with the resale loop as the unproven next leg."
- **Disclosed caveats the article must carry:** (i) Aura scale figures are self-reported, single-source (#22); (ii) no evidence of registry queries at resale; (iii) EU-compliance-substitution risk; (iv) the Zara/Inditex KPI folklore (#10) stays out of load-bearing use.
- **What evidence would settle it:** → research tasks: independent confirmation of Aura adoption depth; any documented resale-marketplace integration with Aura/DPP registries; the final text of the ESPR textiles delegated act (does it require verifiable provenance or accept self-declared databases?).
