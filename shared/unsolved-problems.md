# Master List: "Impossible Until AI" Problems

Cross-domain registry. Each entry links to the domain folder where it's analyzed in depth.

| Problem | Domain | Binding constraint | AI removes it? | Status | Deep dive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keeping E2E UI tests current as apps evolve | Quality engineering | Scripts encode implementation, not intent | Yes — intelligence lives in intent understanding | Solved (reference case) | framework/two-hills-framework.md |
| Overproduction & inventory waste | Fashion | Slow feedback loop between demand signal and shelf | Mostly no as deployed (better forecast, same slow cycle) — unconfirmed exception for vertically-integrated players | Automation in disguise (mixed, needs verification) | domains/fashion/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Authentication & counterfeiting at resale scale | Fashion | Expert attention doesn't scale; authenticity only inferable after the fact | Yes, when identity is assigned at manufacture (Aura Consortium) — no, when CV just automates post-hoc inspection (Entrupy) | Genuine invention candidate (provenance-at-origin); automation in disguise (post-hoc CV) | domains/fashion/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Fit & body-diverse sizing | Fashion | No scalable way to capture individual body data cheaply | Partially — real return-rate reduction, but data representativeness unverified and design-loop not yet closed | Promising but unproven | domains/fashion/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Resale-side provenance verification (the "last mile") | Fashion | Provenance ground truth now exists at manufacture (Aura, 40M+ items; EU DPP mandate ~2028) — but no evidence anyone queries it where secondhand counterfeiting actually lives: thrift, consignment, resale marketplaces | Constraint already removed upstream; the unbuilt piece is the query/verification layer at the point of resale — an integration and adoption problem, not a research problem | **Open opportunity** — flagged for problem solvers/investors (2026-08, from the fashion article's grill session) | domains/fashion/01-brainstorm/grill-me-log.md |
| Simultaneous auction transparency for buy- and sell-side | Ad servers | The party operating the auction holds a structural information advantage it has no incentive to surrender (adjudicated: US v. Google 2025; EU €2.95B fine) | No — AI reporting copilots make past decisions more legible, and "agent-to-agent" protocols turn out to be discovery-then-approve on unchanged RTB (no negotiation step in the specs) | Automation in disguise | domains/ad-servers/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Cross-platform identity resolution without cookies | Ad servers | Coordination/consent problem across parties who won't share data — not a technical resolution problem; the browser-native fix (Privacy Sandbox) failed on adoption | No — solved by consent and acquisition (UID2, LiveRamp/Publicis), with AI as the access interface on top; TTD's moat downgrade shows open standards branded "proprietary" aren't the exclusive asset | Automation in disguise | domains/ad-servers/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Ad fraud adapting faster than detection generalizes | Ad servers | Adversarial adaptation speed; the defense's tooling advantage vanishes once both sides hold similar AI | No — by the defense's own telemetry the offense accelerates faster (AI-agent traffic +7,851% YoY); response is triage/labeling, not elimination | Automation in disguise (losing the acceleration race) | domains/ad-servers/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Independent incrementality measurement (the ad server grades its own homework) | Ad servers | The engine that buys the media also generates the metrics used to judge it; rigorous holdout testing is slow, costly, and against the platform's interest | No — the one independent study found (640 Meta geo-lift tests) contradicts platform self-reporting; no standardized external-audit infrastructure exists | **Open opportunity** — an "external auditor" layer for AI-optimized ad spend, analogous to financial audit (2026-08, from the ad-servers analysis) | domains/ad-servers/00-research/unsolved-problems.md |
| Consumer-side agent consortia (individuals as sellers) | Ad servers | Individuals were always inventory, never parties — no mechanism existed for millions of people to negotiate collectively over their own attention/data | In principle yes: the same agent protocols now standardizing for institutions could aggregate individual agents into negotiating blocs — no vendor in the 112-source evidence base is building it | **Authors' original proposal** (Sudhir Patavardhan, 2026-08) — forward-looking, deliberately not validated against market precedent | domains/ad-servers/01-brainstorm/grill-me-log.md |
