# Pre-Publish Checklist

## Evidence bar
- [ ] Every quantitative claim traces to a numbered entry in the domain's sources.md
- [ ] No claim rests solely on vendor marketing (V-tier sources support "vendors claim X" only)
- [ ] At least 2 primary (P) or report (R) tier sources anchor the central thesis
- [ ] All data points carry dates; nothing older than ~18 months presented as "current"
- [ ] The falsification check was actually run and its result is stated or reflected

## Framework integrity
- [ ] The 2x2 is applied, not just name-dropped
- [ ] The "binding constraint" of the unsolved problem is named explicitly
- [ ] The piece survives its own grill-me log — top 3 attacks are pre-empted in the text
- [ ] Ends with the diagnostic question adapted to the domain

## Craft
- [ ] Automation value acknowledged honestly before being reframed (no strawman)
- [ ] One concrete case study, not a survey
- [ ] A skeptical practitioner in the domain would not find an obvious factual error
