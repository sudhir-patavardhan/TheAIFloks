How we work

Authority from evidence discipline, not headcount

01

One framework

Two Hills — automation vs. invention, individual vs. institutional — applied the same way to every domain. One thesis per industry, not a vendor-rated quadrant.

02

Every claim sourced

A numbered, dated, tiered evidence log ships alongside every report — and is browsable on its own as that domain's Research Materials.

03

Adversarially tested

Theses are grilled before publication. Arguments that survive graduate to the analysis; arguments that die are kept as the objection-handling record.

04

Free, for now

No paywall at launch. Access is email-capture only, the same model the closest comparable reports use while they're building a track record.
